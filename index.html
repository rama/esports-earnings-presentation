NPower Canada · Data Analytics Capstone

Esports Earnings Analytics

From raw data to a visual story about prize money, games, players and regions.

01 · Business Problem

Esports is global. The data is complex.

Our challenge was to turn player-level earnings data into insights that are easy for stakeholders to understand.

02 · Objective

Turn raw data into decisions.

  • Compare prize money across games and genres.
  • Understand geographic distribution.
  • Identify top players and competitive segments.
  • Create an interactive analytical experience.
03 · Data & Technology

Python → Analysis → Dashboard

Python · Pandas · NumPy · Plotly · Dash

Workflow: Ingest → Clean → Analyze → Visualize → Decide

04 · Finding

Prize money is concentrated.

A relatively small group of top-tier games captures a large share of the esports prize economy.

05 · Finding

Genre and geography matter.

Different genres and regions contribute differently to the overall prize landscape.

06 · Recommendations

Make the analysis actionable.

  • Focus on high-value game and genre ecosystems.
  • Use geographic comparisons to understand opportunity.
  • Track changes as new esports data becomes available.
  • Use interactive filters to drill into records.
07 · Conclusion

Data → insight → action.

Our project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow that turns raw esports data into an accessible decision-support story.
